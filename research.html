Research

Four connected threads, each building on the one before it: engineering the data, reading the trends, reconstructing what is missing, and resolving the surface at the scale where change actually happens.

01

Satellite Data Engineering

Processing massive satellite imagery, implementing robust quality-control pipelines, and constructing global-scale geospatial datasets.

I engineered the data processing pipelines for GLCD250-MOD, a daily 250 m global lake dataset covering 465,966 inland waterbodies from 2000 to 2024. Constructing this dataset required building parallel acquisition and processing workflows to handle hundreds of terabytes of multi-sensor satellite imagery, executing automated polygon-based region-of-interest (ROI) extraction, and ensuring spatial–temporal consistency across the continuous global record.

02

Spatiotemporal Trend Analysis

Analyzing long-term geospatial variability, identifying surface trends, and modeling climate-driven dynamics.

Using statistical frameworks like Mann-Kendall trend tests and spatial regime classification, I study how surface observations vary across regional and global scales. My research focuses on analyzing how land surface and water dynamics respond to climate drivers such as drought, examining how these physical responses differ based on climate zones and local basin morphology.

03

Spatiotemporal Deep Learning

Designing neural network architectures for geospatial gap filling, reconstruction, and predictive spatial modeling.

My work focuses on developing deep learning architectures to resolve missing observations in satellite data. By leveraging self-attention mechanisms and spatiotemporal neural networks, I develop frameworks for geospatial gap filling and reconstruction to recover continuous physical series from fragmented or mixed-frequency satellite observations.

04

Geospatial Computer Vision

Leveraging semantic segmentation and object detection for automated feature extraction, crop mapping, and surface change detection.

While coarse-resolution datasets capture macro-scale trends, my vision is to push toward the fine-scale end of the problem. I aim to apply advanced computer vision frameworks to automate agricultural crop mapping, extract precise field boundaries, and track continuous land surface changes at the resolution where physical dynamics actually occur.
